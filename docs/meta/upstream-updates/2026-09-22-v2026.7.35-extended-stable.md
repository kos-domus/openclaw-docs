## Upstream official confirmation (closes the classification debate)

The release body states verbatim: **"OpenClaw 2026.7.33 and 2026.7.34 were unstable extended-stable builds and were intentionally not published as GitHub Releases."** The 404s and rollbacks observed Sep 19-21 were therefore intentional withholding of unstable builds, not registry/GH propagation failures. Our lane-instability classification (2026-09-21 artifact) is verified correct by upstream itself.
